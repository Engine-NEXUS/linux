Privacy Policy for NEXUS Assistant

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Data Minimization & Local Privacy

NEXUS Assistant is designed as an on-device personal assistant. All user data, speech transcripts, configuration settings, and OAuth access/refresh tokens are stored locally on your device within the OS Credential Store (Windows Credential Manager / macOS Keychain).

2. Google User Data Protection

When you authenticate with your Google Account via RFC 8252 Loopback OAuth, NEXUS requests permissions solely for reading email updates (for proactive deadline alerts), accessing calendar events, searching photos, and maps routing.

  • Google tokens are stored exclusively in your local OS Keychain.
  • Google user data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to, sold to, or shared with third-party remote servers.

3. Telemetry & Analytics

NEXUS Assistant does not collect, transmit, or sell personal telemetry, user data, or analytics to any external tracking servers.
